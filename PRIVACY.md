# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 16, 2026

**Daily Opportunities Hub** ("we", "our", or "us") operates the automated social media manager system (**Social-Medial-Manager**). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you interact with our automated content feeds and pages.

## 1. Information We Collect
Our automated system collects publicly available job listings, local news updates, and world news from trusted public sources (such as RemoteOK, We Work Remotely, MyJoyOnline, GNA, and BBC News) for the purpose of sharing professional opportunities and verified news updates with our community. We do not collect or store private personal user data.

## 2. How We Use Information
- To aggregate and summarize career opportunities and public news.
- To publish informative updates to our official social media pages (Facebook and TikTok queue).

## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure
We do not sell, trade, or transfer public content or user information to outside parties.
